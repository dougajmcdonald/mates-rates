# Mates Rates — Marketing Playbook

## Product in One Line
The private marketplace where families and friends sell to people they actually know — no strangers, no no-shows, no nonsense.

## Target Audience
**Primary:** Parents and suburban families — decluttering kids' gear, upgrading furniture, moving house. They have large items, established social circles, and a deep mistrust of strangers coming to their door.

**Pain they feel most:**
- Strangers coming to their home to collect a sofa, wardrobe, or kids' bed
- No-shows after taking time off work or rearranging the day
- Selling to the first random person who messages rather than someone they'd trust
- WhatsApp group chats pinging at all hours to ask "is this still available?"

## Tone of Voice
Warm, honest, slightly wry. You're a parent or homeowner who's been burned before. You built this because you needed it. Never corporate, never salesy. Talk like you're telling a mate at the school gate.

---

## Facebook (60-Day Plan)

**Posting cadence:** 3× per week (Mon / Wed / Fri)
**Optimal post length:** 150–300 words. Storytelling posts outperform short posts in the family/community audience segment.
**Images:** Use your phone. Real photos of real items in real homes dramatically outperform stock. If you don't have a photo, use an emoji-heavy text post.
**Boost budget:** £5–10 on high-performing posts after Day 21 once you see what resonates.

**Subject:** What makes a good offer?

> Hi,
>
> A few tips if you're making offers on listings (not just selling):
>
> **Be straightforward.** "I'd like to take this for £X if it's still available — can collect [day]."
>
> **Don't low-ball.** You know the person, or know someone who does. Fair offers get accepted. Cheeky ones don't.
>
> **Be ready to collect.** Sellers remember who was easy to deal with. If you say you'll collect Thursday, collect Thursday.
>
> This is second-hand selling with social accountability. Treat it like you'd treat a favour between mates.
>
> Doug

## Signup Notification (Technical)

The server now sends an email to **dougajmcdonald@gmail.com** whenever a new user signs up.

### Setup Required

1. **Create a free Resend account** at [resend.com](https://resend.com) (3,000 emails/month free)
2. **Get your API key**: Resend Dashboard → API Keys → Create API Key
3. **Add to your server environment variables:**

```bash
# apps/server/.env (local)
RESEND_API_KEY=re_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

# Vercel → Settings → Environment Variables
RESEND_API_KEY=re_xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
```

4. **Redeploy** the server after adding the env var.

### How It Works

The `/api/users/sync` endpoint (called every time someone logs in) now detects new signups by checking whether the user's `createdAt` timestamp is within the last 30 seconds.

- **New signup** → sends email notification to you
- **Returning user logging in** → no email, just syncs the database

### Upgrade: Use Your Own Domain

Once you have 50+ signups and want a branded from address (`notifications@mates-rates.org`):
1. Resend Dashboard → Domains → Add Domain
2. Add the 3 DNS records to your domain registrar
3. Change the `from` field in `app.ts` from `onboarding@resend.dev` to `Mates Rates <notifications@mates-rates.org>`
